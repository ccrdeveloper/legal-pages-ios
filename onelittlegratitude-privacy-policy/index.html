Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how One Little Gratitude: Thankful (“the App”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) handles information when you use the App. The App is developed by Syahrul Ahmad Saputra.

One Little Gratitude: Thankful is designed to help users build a gentle gratitude habit through daily gratitude quotes, gratitude challenges, reflection prompts, journaling, reminders, progress tracking, and related app features.

1. Information We Collect

The App may allow you to create or save personal content, such as gratitude notes, completed challenges, favorite quotes, reminder settings, streaks, progress data, and app preferences.

This information is used to provide the App’s core features and improve your personal app experience.

2. Personal Journal and Reflection Content

Gratitude notes, reflections, and journal entries you create in the App are intended for your personal use. We do not ask you to enter sensitive personal information, and you should avoid writing information that you do not want to store on your device.

3. Local Data Storage

The App may store your gratitude notes, completed challenges, favorites, progress, streaks, and settings locally on your device. If your device or operating system provides backup services, some App data may be included in your device backup depending on your device settings.

4. Notifications

The App may request permission to send local notifications, such as gratitude reminders or daily reflection reminders. Notifications are optional and can be enabled, disabled, or changed in your device settings.

5. Purchases and Subscriptions

The App may offer premium features through in-app purchases or subscriptions. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or store your payment card details.

The App may use a third-party purchase management service, such as RevenueCat, to help manage subscription status, purchase validation, trial eligibility, and premium access. Such services may process purchase-related identifiers and transaction information needed to provide premium features.

6. Analytics and Diagnostics

The App may collect limited analytics or diagnostic information to understand app performance, improve features, fix bugs, and maintain reliability. This may include general usage events, device type, operating system version, crash information, and performance data.

We do not use analytics to read the personal content of your gratitude journal entries.

7. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services to support features such as analytics, app performance, purchase management, or crash diagnostics. These services may process limited information according to their own privacy policies.

Examples may include Apple App Store services, RevenueCat, Firebase Analytics, or similar services used for app functionality and improvement.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your gratitude journal content with advertisers. Information may only be shared when needed to operate the App, comply with legal obligations, prevent fraud, process purchases, or protect the rights and safety of users and the developer.

9. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under the age required by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

10. Data Retention

Data stored locally in the App may remain on your device until you delete it, reset the App, or uninstall the App. Purchase-related records may be retained by Apple or purchase management services as required for transaction history, subscription validation, tax, accounting, or legal purposes.

11. Your Choices

You may manage or delete certain App data through the App features, device settings, or by uninstalling the App. You may also disable notifications in your device settings.

Subscription management, cancellation, refunds, and billing are handled through your Apple ID and App Store account settings.

12. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information associated with the App. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and account.
