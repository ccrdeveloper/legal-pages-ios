Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 2, 2026

Welcome to One Little Declutter. Your privacy is of utmost importance to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information and data.

TL;DR: One Little Declutter is designed around a local-first philosophy. Your personal decluttering entries, logs, notes, and emotional mood states are stored locally on your device and are never sent to any external servers.

1. Information Collection & Use

Local User Content

All items you log, categories you choose, notes you write, dates completed, and emotional reflection check-ins (before and after states) are stored locally in the application's sandbox using CoreData and secure local device storage.

  • We do not host or operate servers that store your personal decluttering logs or written notes.
  • We do not have access to, read, or distribute your custom logs or personal notes.
  • Deleting the app from your device will permanently erase this local data unless you have a device-level backup (like an iCloud device backup).

App Settings & Preferences

Preferences such as your greeting name, notification reminders, sound settings, haptic settings, and app themes are stored locally on your device using standard OS preferences (UserDefaults) and shared only within your device's App Group for widget sync.

2. Third-Party Services & Integrations

We work with trusted third-party providers to facilitate specific technical actions in the app:

  • RevenueCat: We use RevenueCat to process and validate in-app subscription purchases (One Little Declutter Pro). RevenueCat maps subscription entitlements using anonymous, random user identifiers. No personal decluttering notes, names, or mood data are shared with RevenueCat.
  • App Store / StoreKit: Transactions and subscription billing are handled directly by Apple's App Store. We do not collect or store your payment details, credit card numbers, or billing addresses.

3. Usage Analytics (Optional)

If you choose to keep analytics enabled, the app collects anonymous telemetry events (such as tracking when a challenge is marked complete, when a quote is favorited, or when onboarding is completed) to help us improve the user experience.

  • These events do not contain any personal identifier or custom text you write.
  • You can disable analytics tracking at any time by toggling "Share Usage Analytics" off in the Settings screen.

4. Notification Data

Daily reminder alerts and re-engagement notifications are scheduled and processed locally on your device via iOS UserNotifications. We do not use remote push servers, and no notifications transmit your declutter details or activity history off-device.

5. Children's Privacy

One Little Declutter does not target or knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since all custom entries and logs are stored solely on your device, we do not compile databases of children's data.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the effective date above.
