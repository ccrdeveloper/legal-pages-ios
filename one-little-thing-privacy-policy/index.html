Privacy Policy for One Little Thing

Last updated: May 24, 2026

One Little Thing is a kindness habit app. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

If you have questions, contact us at ccrdevbusiness@gmail.com.

Information Stored on Your Device

One Little Thing stores your habit data locally on your device. This may include:

  • Your optional display name for in-app greetings
  • Kindness completions, streaks, points, badges, and progress history
  • Mood check-ins before and after a kindness activity
  • Optional reflections and custom kindness text you enter
  • Favorite quotes and favorite challenges
  • App settings such as language, theme, sound, haptics, reminders, analytics preference, and widget preferences
  • Widget data such as streak, challenge, mood history, reflections, and progress summaries, stored in the app group so the widget can display them

Reflection text and custom kindness text are not sent to Firebase, RevenueCat, iCloud, CloudKit, or a developer-owned backend by this app. If you delete the app, locally stored data may be deleted from your device.

One Little Thing is not a medical, mental health, therapy, diagnosis, or treatment app. Please do not enter sensitive medical or health information into reflections or custom text fields.

Analytics

If analytics are enabled, One Little Thing uses Firebase Analytics to understand app usage and improve the app. Analytics may include events such as app opens, screen visits, completed challenges, selected challenge categories, reminder settings, premium feature interactions, and general app settings.

The app is designed not to send reflection text, custom kindness text, email addresses, names, contacts, precise location, medical data, or health data as analytics event parameters. For reflections, the app may send only a length bucket, such as empty, short, medium, or long.

Firebase Analytics may process technical information such as device and app information, usage events, diagnostics, and identifiers used by Firebase to provide analytics. You can turn analytics off in the app settings. When analytics are off, the app disables Firebase Analytics collection.

Purchases and Subscriptions

One Little Thing may offer premium features through Apple in-app purchases and subscriptions. Purchases are processed by Apple. The app uses RevenueCat to manage subscription status, restore purchases, and determine whether premium features are active.

We do not receive or store your payment card information. Apple handles payment processing. RevenueCat may process purchase-related information such as product identifiers, transaction status, entitlement status, app user identifiers generated by the purchase system, and related subscription metadata.

Notifications

If you choose to enable reminders, One Little Thing asks for notification permission and schedules local notifications, such as daily kindness reminders or re-engagement reminders. You can change notification permissions in iOS Settings and app reminder settings.

Photos

If you choose to save quote images, the app may request add-only access to your photo library. This permission is used to save the image you request. The app does not need to read your full photo library for this feature.

Support and Feedback

If you contact us by email for support or feedback, we may receive your email address and the information you choose to include in your message. We use that information only to respond to you, troubleshoot issues, and improve the app.

How Information Is Used

Information handled by the app is used to:

  • Provide kindness challenges, reflections, progress, streaks, widgets, and app settings
  • Show optional local reminders
  • Provide premium subscription features and restore purchases
  • Understand app usage and improve app quality when analytics are enabled
  • Respond to support and feedback requests

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

Information may be processed by service providers only as needed to operate the app:

  • Firebase Analytics, for optional analytics
  • RevenueCat, for subscription and entitlement management
  • Apple, for App Store distribution, in-app purchases, subscriptions, notifications permissions, and photo library permissions

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users, the app, or others.

Your Choices

You can:

  • Disable analytics in the app settings
  • Disable notifications in the app settings or iOS Settings
  • Reset progress in the app settings
  • Delete the app to remove locally stored app data from your device, subject to iOS behavior and backups
  • Manage subscriptions through your Apple ID subscription settings
  • Contact us at ccrdevbusiness@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests

Children

One Little Thing is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and respond.

International Users

Your information may be processed in the country where you live and in other countries where our service providers operate. Privacy laws may differ by country.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date and, where appropriate, provide additional notice.
