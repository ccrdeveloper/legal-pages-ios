Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RepairWise AI (“the App,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), developed by Syahrul Ahmad Saputra, collects, uses, and protects information when you use the App.

By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Overview

RepairWise AI is an English-only, safety-first AI repair guidance app for simple everyday household issues.

The App may use photos, typed descriptions, and voice-to-text descriptions to generate AI-guided repair suggestions.

AI suggestions are for general guidance only and are not a replacement for qualified professionals.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may process the following information:

a. Text Descriptions

If you type a repair issue, the App may process the text you provide to generate AI guidance.

Examples include descriptions such as: “The door is sagging and the hinge looks loose.”

b. Photos

If you use camera scan or photo upload, the App may process the selected photo to generate AI guidance.

Photos are used only for repair guidance. You should avoid uploading sensitive personal information, faces, private documents, addresses, financial details, medical information, or any content you do not want processed.

c. Voice Descriptions

If voice input is available and you choose to use it, the App may use microphone and speech recognition features to convert your voice into text.

The resulting text may be used to generate AI guidance.

The App does not need to store raw audio to provide repair guidance.

d. Repair History

If you save an AI result, the App may store repair history on your device.

Saved history may include:

  • issue title
  • date
  • safety level
  • AI guidance result
  • optional photo thumbnail
  • optional user notes

Repair history is intended to be stored locally on your device unless a future version clearly states otherwise.

e. Subscription Information

We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and purchases.

RevenueCat may process purchase-related information such as:

  • subscription status
  • product identifier
  • purchase date
  • renewal status
  • app user identifier generated for subscription management

We do not receive your full payment card details.

Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store.

f. Basic Analytics Data

We use Firebase Analytics to understand basic app usage and improve the App.

Analytics events may include:

  • onboarding started or completed
  • paywall shown or dismissed
  • purchase started, completed, failed, or restored
  • analysis started, completed, or failed
  • safety level returned
  • free analysis limit reached
  • history saved
  • settings opened

Analytics may include general technical information such as device type, app version, operating system version, approximate region, and usage events.

3. Information We Do Not Log in Analytics

We do not use analytics to log:

  • photos
  • audio recordings
  • voice transcripts
  • full repair descriptions
  • full AI results
  • personal repair notes
  • exact home address
  • precise location
  • sensitive personal content

4. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • provide AI-generated repair guidance
  • analyze photos or descriptions you submit
  • classify safety level
  • show suggested first steps
  • save repair history if you choose to save it
  • manage premium access and subscriptions
  • restore purchases
  • improve app performance and user experience
  • monitor basic app usage through privacy-conscious analytics
  • prevent abuse and maintain app functionality

5. AI Processing

RepairWise AI uses an AI service provider through a backend/proxy system.

When you request AI guidance, your text description and optional photo may be sent to the AI service provider only to generate repair guidance.

The App is designed to avoid sending unnecessary personal information. You should not submit sensitive personal content.

AI-generated responses may be incomplete or inaccurate. For electrical, gas, fire, structural, battery, or high-risk issues, contact a qualified professional.

6. Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services:

a. RevenueCat

Used for subscription management, purchase status, and restore purchases.

b. Firebase Analytics

Used for basic app usage analytics.

c. OpenRouter / AI Service Provider

Used to generate AI repair guidance from text descriptions and optional photos.

These third-party services may process data according to their own privacy policies.

7. Camera, Photo Library, Microphone, and Speech Recognition

The App may request access to:

  • Camera: Used to take photos of household repair issues.
  • Photo Library: Used to select photos for AI repair guidance.
  • Microphone: Used only if voice input is available and you choose to describe an issue by voice.
  • Speech Recognition: Used to convert your voice description into text.

You can manage permissions in your iOS device settings.

8. Local Storage

Repair history may be stored locally on your device.

If you delete the App, locally stored history may be deleted.

The App may also store basic settings locally, such as:

  • onboarding completion
  • safety agreement acceptance
  • free analysis usage count
  • saved repair history
  • premium access cache

9. Subscriptions and Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store.

We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active and to unlock premium features.

We do not collect or store your full payment card information.

10. Data Retention

Locally saved repair history remains on your device until you delete it or delete the App.

Analytics data may be retained by Firebase according to Firebase settings and policies.

Subscription data may be retained by RevenueCat as needed to manage purchases and subscription status.

AI request data may be processed by the AI service provider according to its policies.

11. Data Deletion

You may delete saved repair history inside the App if the feature is available.

You may also delete the App to remove locally stored app data from your device.
