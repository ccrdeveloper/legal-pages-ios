Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 2, 2026
Last Updated: June 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how One Little Prayer: Bible Verse ("we," "us," or "the App"), operated by Syahrul Ahmad Saputra, handles information when you use our iOS application.

We designed One Little Prayer: Bible Verse to be a private, local-first experience. There is no account, no sign-in, no cloud sync, and no advertising. This policy is intentionally short because we collect almost nothing.

Developer: Syahrul Ahmad Saputra
Email: ccrdevbusiness@gmail.com

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any contact information
  • Your prayers, reflections, mood check-ins, custom prayer text, or prayer history, which are stored only on your device
  • Your location, precise or approximate
  • Your contacts, calendar, or other personal device data
  • Any data for advertising or marketing tracking
  • Any device identifier for cross-app tracking. We do not request permission via App Tracking Transparency because we do not track.

We do not use third-party analytics SDKs in this version of the App.

2. Information We Do Collect

2.1 Purchase and Subscription Data

When you purchase a Premium subscription, the transaction is processed by Apple through the App Store and your subscription status is verified by RevenueCat, Inc., our subscription-management provider.

We receive:

  • An anonymous user identifier generated by RevenueCat
  • Your active subscription status, such as active, lapsed, or canceled
  • The product ID purchased, such as monthly or yearly Premium
  • The country of the App Store account for tax and pricing purposes

We do not receive your Apple ID, credit-card information, or billing address. Apple handles all payment processing.

2.2 Crash Diagnostics

If you have Share With App Developers enabled in your iOS Privacy settings, Apple may share anonymous crash logs and performance diagnostics with us. These contain no personally identifying information.

You can disable this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements on your device.

3. Information Stored Only on Your Device

The following data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to share it, for example by saving a quote card to your Photos library:

  • Your daily prayer entries and timestamps
  • Your mood check-ins before and after each prayer
  • Your written reflections and custom prayers
  • Your saved favorite verses and prompts
  • Your streak, points, and progress statistics
  • Your app preferences, such as language, theme, haptics, and sound
  • Custom photo background you select for quote cards

This data is stored in your device's local storage and in an App Group container shared between the main app and its Home Screen widget. Deleting the App removes all of this data permanently.

4. How We Use Information

We use the limited information described in Section 2 only to:

  • Verify your Premium subscription is active
  • Unlock Premium features on your device
  • Restore your purchases when you reinstall the App
  • Fix bugs reported through Apple's crash diagnostics

We do not sell, rent, or share any information with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Third-Party Services

5.1 Apple

Subscriptions are processed by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy.

5.2 RevenueCat

We use RevenueCat to manage and verify subscription status. RevenueCat receives only the anonymous identifier described in Section 2.1. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy for details.

We have no other third-party data processors.

6. Photo Library Access

The App requests access to your Photo Library in two situations:

  • Read access — only when you tap "Use Custom Photo" to choose your own background for quote cards. We import only the photo you select.
  • Write access — only when you tap "Save" on a quote card to add it to your Photos library.

You may revoke this permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → One Little Prayer: Bible Verse. The App functions normally without Photo Library access; you simply cannot use custom backgrounds or save quote cards.

7. Bible Translations

The Scripture quoted in the App is taken from public-domain translations:

  • English — World English Bible (WEB), public domain
  • Spanish — Reina-Valera 1909 (RV 1909), public domain

We do not collect any data based on which verses you read or favorite. That information remains on your device.

8. Children's Privacy

One Little Prayer: Bible Verse is rated 4+ and is appropriate for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

If you believe a child under 13 has somehow provided us with information, which would require a Premium purchase, please contact us and we will work with Apple to refund and remove the data.

9. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA / Similar Laws)

Because we do not collect personal information that identifies you, there is no personal data for us to provide, correct, or delete on request. Your prayer content lives only on your device — you fully control it by adding, editing, or deleting entries in the App, or by uninstalling the App.

If you live in the European Union, United Kingdom, California, or another jurisdiction with data-privacy laws, you may contact us at the email below with any questions, and we will respond within 30 days.

To delete your local data:

  • Inside the App: clear individual reflections from the Progress tab
  • Wipe everything: uninstall the App on your iOS device

To cancel your subscription:

  • iOS Settings → Your Apple ID → Subscriptions → One Little Prayer: Bible Verse → Cancel

10. Data Security

Because we do not transmit your personal content to any server, the security of your data depends primarily on the security of your device. We recommend keeping iOS updated and using a device passcode.

Subscription verification requests to Apple and RevenueCat use TLS-encrypted connections.

11. International Users

The App is offered worldwide via the Apple App Store. If you use the App outside Indonesia, please note that any information we receive as described in Section 2 is processed by our service providers in the United States and other countries where they operate.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be announced via an in-app notice or in the App Store release notes. Continued use of the App after a change indicates your acceptance of the updated policy.
