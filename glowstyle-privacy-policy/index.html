Privacy Policy

App: GlowStyle Ideas
Developer: Syahrul Ahmad Saputra
Last Updated: June 4, 2026

GlowStyle Ideas ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application, GlowStyle Ideas (the "App").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By accessing or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the App.

Important: GlowStyle Ideas provides cosmetic appearance and personal style insights only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition. For skin concerns or health questions, please consult a qualified professional.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization and transparency. We collect or process only the information needed to provide the App’s core functionality, such as estimating face shape, providing cosmetic appearance insights, managing your Progress Journal, supporting subscriptions, and improving app stability.

A. 2D Photo and Face Image Data

When you take a photo using the App’s camera or select a photo from your device’s photo library, the App accesses the 2D visual image that you choose to provide.

No TrueDepth or ARKit Face Tracking: GlowStyle Ideas does not use or access Apple’s TrueDepth API, ARKit face tracking, ARFaceTrackingConfiguration, 3D facial topology, facial mesh mapping, depth maps, coordinate tracking data, or biometric face prints.

How photos are used: The selected 2D photo may be optimized, resized, and converted into a compatible format on your device before being transmitted securely via encrypted HTTPS to our AI service provider, OpenRouter. OpenRouter may process the image using Gemini and GPT models only to generate non-medical cosmetic and style suggestions, such as face shape estimates, appearance notes, hairstyle ideas, grooming ideas, glasses ideas, photo pose tips, and general self-care ideas.

No face recognition or identification: GlowStyle Ideas does not use face images for facial recognition, user identification, user authentication, profile matching, identity comparison, or tracking. The App does not identify people and does not compare faces to determine identity.

No sensitive trait prediction: GlowStyle Ideas does not predict age, gender, ethnicity, attractiveness, emotions, or other sensitive personal traits.

Data sharing for AI processing: Selected 2D photos are shared only with our AI processing provider, OpenRouter, for the purpose of generating the cosmetic styling result requested by you. Photos are not shared with advertisers, data brokers, or unrelated third parties.

Remote storage: We do not store your face images or facial analysis data on our own servers. Photos are processed for the duration needed to complete the AI analysis request. We do not write your face image data to persistent remote storage controlled by us.

B. Progress Journal Data

If you save scan results, the App may store your saved style summaries, including dates, face shape estimates, appearance notes, style ideas, self-care ideas, and related journal information.

Your Progress Journal is stored locally on your device using iOS persistent local storage, such as UserDefaults or local file storage, depending on the App version. We do not sync your Progress Journal to our own remote server.

The Progress Journal is for private cosmetic review only. It is not medical history and should not be used for diagnosis, treatment, or health decisions.

C. Purchase and Subscription Data

To verify and manage premium access, GlowStyle Ideas may use RevenueCat or a similar subscription management service. RevenueCat may process purchase-related information such as transaction IDs, purchase dates, subscription status, receipt validation tokens, and entitlement status.

Subscription data may be associated with an anonymous or randomized user identifier generated by the App or subscription service. We do not collect or store your real name, billing address, full payment card number, or credit card information. Financial transactions are handled by Apple through the App Store.

D. Device, Usage, and Diagnostic Information

The App may collect limited technical and diagnostic information, such as crash reports, app performance data, device type, operating system version, and feature interaction data. This information is used to maintain app stability, fix bugs, and improve the user experience.

We do not use this information to track your precise location, browsing history, or identity.

E. Contact Information

If you contact us for support, we may receive your email address and any information you choose to include in your message. We use this information only to respond to your request and provide support.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide cosmetic appearance and personal style insights.
  • Generate face shape estimates and beauty/style suggestions from selected photos.
  • Display and save scan results in the Progress Journal when you choose to save them.
  • Manage premium features, subscriptions, entitlements, and purchase restoration.
  • Improve app performance, stability, security, and user experience.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Comply with legal, safety, platform, and App Store requirements.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share information with trusted third-party service providers when necessary to operate the App.

  • OpenRouter: Selected photos may be securely transmitted to OpenRouter to generate cosmetic appearance and style insights using AI models.
  • RevenueCat: Purchase receipts and subscription-related data may be processed by RevenueCat to validate and manage premium access.
  • Apple App Store: Purchases, renewals, cancellations, and refunds are processed by Apple.
  • Analytics or crash reporting tools: If used, limited diagnostic data may be processed to help improve app performance and reliability.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Image Data: Photos captured or uploaded to the App are used to complete the selected cosmetic scan request. We do not store your face images on our own servers. Image data is processed only as needed to generate the requested result.

Progress Journal: Your saved journal entries are stored locally on your device. You have control over this data. If the App provides delete options, you may delete specific journal entries or clear the entire journal from within the App. You may also remove locally stored data by deleting the App from your device.

Subscription History: Subscription records and transaction history may be retained by Apple and RevenueCat as needed for purchase validation, subscription management, legal compliance, and fraud prevention.

5. Your Choices and Controls

You may choose not to upload photos or use the scan features. You may also deny camera or photo library permission through your device settings, although some App features may not work without those permissions.

You may manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple ID subscription settings. You may delete local journal entries where the App provides that option.

6. Camera and Photo Library Permissions

GlowStyle Ideas may request access to your camera and photo library only when needed for app functionality.

  • Camera access: Used to take a face photo for cosmetic appearance and style insights.
  • Photo library access: Used to select an image for cosmetic appearance and style insights.
  • Photo saving access: If available, used only when you choose to save or export a style summary.

7. No Medical, Diagnostic, or Health Use

GlowStyle Ideas is a cosmetic and personal style app. The App does not provide medical advice, medical diagnosis, skin disease detection, clinical results, treatment plans, medication advice, risk scores, or emergency guidance.

AI-generated outputs are intended only for general cosmetic, beauty, grooming, style, and self-care purposes. Results may vary depending on photo quality, lighting, angle, camera settings, and other factors.

For skin concerns, health questions, or medical decisions, please consult a qualified professional.

8. Security of Your Data

We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. Communications between the App and service providers such as OpenRouter and RevenueCat are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children’s Privacy

GlowStyle Ideas is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

10. International Data Processing

Depending on the service providers used by the App, information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live. These countries may have data protection laws that differ from those in your jurisdiction.

11. Apple App Store Compliance

This Privacy Policy is designed to support compliance with Apple App Store Review Guidelines, including privacy transparency requirements. We provide disclosures about photo processing, AI processing, subscriptions, and local Progress Journal storage so users can make informed choices.
