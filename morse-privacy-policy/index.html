Privacy Policy for Morse Learner

Last updated: June 20, 2026

Morse Learner ("we", "our", or "the app") is an educational Android application that helps users learn International Morse code. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and your choices. By using Morse Learner, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Morse Learner does not collect, transmit, or sell personal information. The app works entirely offline and does not require an account.

Data stored locally on your device:

DataPurpose
Speed setting (WPM)Remember your preferred Morse playback speed
Completed lessonsRemember your learning progress

This data stays on your device, is not sent to our servers, and is not shared with third parties.

2. Permissions

The app does not request Internet, location, camera, microphone recording, contacts, or phone permissions. It only plays audio through your speaker.

3. Third-Party Services

No analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or social login SDKs are used.

4. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

5. Data Security & Deletion

All data is stored locally. Delete it via Android Settings → Apps → Morse Learner → Clear data, or uninstall the app.

6. Android Backup

App preferences may be included in your device's encrypted Google backup if enabled on your device.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date will reflect changes.

Questions? Email: ccrdevbusiness@gmail.com

  • Data shared: None
  • Encrypted in transit: Not applicable
