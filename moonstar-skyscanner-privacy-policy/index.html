Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MoonStar: Sky Scanner (“the App”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) handles information when you use our AI-assisted night sky scanner application.

MoonStar: Sky Scanner is developed by Syahrul Ahmad Saputra. The App is designed to help users scan the moon, stars, and night sky for educational and entertainment purposes.

1. Information We May Collect

The App may collect or process the following types of information depending on the features you use:

  • Photos you capture or upload for AI-assisted sky analysis.
  • Scan results, such as estimated moon phase, sky clarity, star visibility, and image quality.
  • Optional journal entries or notes you save in the App.
  • Device-related information, such as app version, device type, and general diagnostic data.
  • Approximate or precise location only if you grant permission and only when needed for sky-related features.
  • Purchase or subscription status when using premium features.

2. Photo and Image Processing

When you scan or upload a night sky photo, the image may be processed by AI services to generate sky insights. These insights may include estimated moon phase, detected sky objects, star visibility, sky clarity, cloud obstruction, light pollution estimate, image quality, and photography tips.

The App does not use your photos to identify you personally. However, you should avoid uploading images that contain private, sensitive, or personally identifiable information.

3. AI-Assisted Results

MoonStar: Sky Scanner provides AI-assisted results for educational and entertainment purposes only. Results are estimates and may vary depending on image quality, camera performance, weather, light pollution, location, and other conditions.

The App is not a professional astronomy, scientific measurement, navigation, or research tool.

4. Location Information

Some features may request access to your location to provide more relevant sky-related information, such as moon visibility, moon calendar context, or stargazing insights.

Location access is optional. You can allow, deny, or disable location access at any time through your device settings.

5. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Provide AI-assisted night sky scan results.
  • Generate moon, star, and sky visibility insights.
  • Save your scan history or journal entries if you choose to use those features.
  • Improve app performance, reliability, and user experience.
  • Manage premium access, subscriptions, or purchases.
  • Respond to support requests.

6. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services for features such as AI image processing, analytics, crash reporting, purchases, subscriptions, or app functionality. These services may process data according to their own privacy policies.

Examples of third-party services may include:

  • AI processing providers
  • Apple services
  • RevenueCat for subscription management
  • Firebase or similar services for analytics or diagnostics, if enabled

7. Subscriptions and Purchases

Premium features may be offered through in-app purchases or subscriptions. Payment and subscription management are handled by Apple and/or subscription management services. We do not directly collect or store your payment card information.

8. Data Storage

Some data, such as scan history, journal entries, or saved results, may be stored locally on your device. If you delete the App, locally stored data may also be deleted unless backup or sync features are available and enabled.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information only when necessary to operate the App, process AI results, manage purchases, comply with legal obligations, prevent fraud, or protect the safety and integrity of the App.

10. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under the age required by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

11. Your Choices

You may:

  • Choose not to upload photos.
  • Delete saved journal entries or scan results if the App provides this option.
  • Disable camera or photo access in your device settings.
  • Disable location access in your device settings.
  • Manage subscriptions through your Apple account settings.
