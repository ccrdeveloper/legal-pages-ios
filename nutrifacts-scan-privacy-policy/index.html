Privacy Policy for NutriFacts Scan AI

Last Updated: May 31, 2026

NutriFacts Scan AI ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application, NutriFacts Scan AI (the "App").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By accessing or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access the App.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization and transparency. We collect the minimum amount of data required to provide the core functionality of the App: analyzing nutrition labels and managing your subscriptions.

A. Photo & Image Data (For Analysis Only)

  • What we collect: When you use the App's camera to scan a nutrition label or upload a photo from your library, we temporarily access and process the image.
  • How it is used: The image is optimized, converted to a base64 string, and transmitted securely via HTTPS to the OpenRouter API, utilizing the Google Gemini model, to perform optical character recognition (OCR) and generate a nutrition summary.
  • Storage: We do not store your images on our servers. Images are only processed in memory for the duration of the API call.

B. Local Scan History (On-Device Storage)

  • What we collect: The App stores your scan reports, including dates, simple summaries, nutrition highlights, and analysis JSON, locally on your device.
  • Storage: This data is stored using iOS persistent local storage, including UserDefaults and file systems, and remains on your device. We do not sync your scan history to a remote server. You can delete individual scans or clear your entire history at any time from the App's settings screen.

C. Purchase and Subscription Data

  • What we collect: To verify and manage your premium subscription, we use a third-party service, RevenueCat. RevenueCat collects purchase-related information such as transaction IDs, purchase dates, subscription status, and receipt validation tokens.
  • Identity: This data is associated with an anonymous, randomized user identifier generated by the App to protect your identity. We do not collect or store your real name, email address, billing address, or credit card information. All financial transactions are handled securely by Apple's App Store.

D. Device and Usage Information

  • What we collect: We do not track your location, browsing history, or personal identifiers. The App may collect anonymous diagnostic logs, such as crash reports, to help us maintain stability and troubleshoot bugs.

2. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share information with trusted third-party service providers who are essential to the App's operations:

  1. OpenRouter (AI Analysis): Image data is securely transmitted to OpenRouter to analyze and extract nutrition facts using the Google Gemini model. This processing is subject to OpenRouter's privacy policy.
  2. RevenueCat (Subscription Management): Transaction receipts are securely sent to RevenueCat to check and validate your entitlement status. This processing is subject to RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  3. Apple App Store (Payment Processing): All subscription purchases, renewals, and refunds are processed exclusively by Apple. Your financial details are subject to Apple’s Privacy Policy.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Image Data: Photos captured or uploaded to the App are deleted automatically from our operational memory immediately after the analysis request completes.
  • Scan History: Your scan history is stored locally on your device. You have full control over this data. You can delete specific scans or purge the entire scan database at any time using the "Clear Scan History" option in the Settings view.
  • Subscription History: Subscription tokens are stored by Apple and RevenueCat. If you wish to delete your anonymous subscription history, you may request help through our support channel.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete that information as quickly as possible.

5. Security of Your Data

We take the security of your data seriously. All communications between the App and our service providers, including OpenRouter and RevenueCat, are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). However, please note that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Apple App Store Compliance

This Privacy Policy is designed to comply with Apple’s App Store Review Guidelines, including Guideline 5.1 (Privacy). We respect user privacy choices, provide transparent disclosures, and grant you full control over your locally stored scan data.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
