💳 Subscriptions

How do I cancel my subscription?

  1. Open iPhone Settings
  2. Tap your name at the top
  3. Tap Subscriptions
  4. Select One Little Thing
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription

Your premium features remain active until the end of the billing period.

How do I restore a previous purchase?

Open the app → Settings → tap Restore Purchases.

Free vs Premium

The app is fully usable for free. Premium unlocks the full kindness library, all daily quotes, 15 badges, 7 large widgets, custom kindness creation, and Aurora widget theme.

🔒 Privacy

Where is my data stored?

All your moods, reflections, streaks, and progress are stored only on your device — never uploaded to our servers.

What does the app collect?

Only anonymous usage events, such as which features you tap and which screens you view, via Firebase Analytics. You can disable this in Settings → Privacy → Analytics.

📱 Widgets

How do I add a widget?

  • Home Screen: long-press an empty area → tap + → search "One Little Thing" → choose size → Add.
  • Lock Screen: long-press Lock Screen → Customize → Lock Screen → tap widget area → choose One Little Thing.

Widget isn't updating

Open the app once to refresh. Widgets update automatically every few hours, or whenever you mark a kindness as done.
