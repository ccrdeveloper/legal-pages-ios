App Purpose

Luxury Bag Price Identifier is an AI-powered bag scanner and valuation assistant. The App helps users identify visible luxury bag details and receive estimated market value ranges based on available online market references.

Photos and AI Processing

When you capture or upload a photo of a designer bag for analysis, the photo is sent securely over HTTPS to our AI service provider, OpenRouter, only to generate the requested bag identification and pricing report.

We do not use the App to identify private people, perform face recognition, predict age, gender, ethnicity, or authenticate identity. Avoid uploading photos that contain sensitive personal information.

The App may highlight visible authenticity indicators, but it does NOT verify or guarantee authenticity.

Information Stored on Your Device

The App may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Saved bag scan reports and details
  • Photos attached to saved history or collection items
  • Notes, favorites, dates, and scan history
  • App preferences and onboarding status
  • Free scan count, premium status, and related subscription state

You can clear saved scan history from the App settings when that option is available.

Purchases and Subscriptions

The App may offer premium features such as unlimited AI scans (subject to a fair use limit of 50 scans/month), detailed reports, full history access, collection tracking, and sharing. Purchases and subscriptions are processed through Apple and managed with RevenueCat. We do not directly collect or store your full payment card details.

How We Use Information

We use information only to provide and improve requested App features, including:

  • Generating AI-assisted designer bag reports and price estimates
  • Saving scan history and wishlist items on your device
  • Managing free scan limits and premium access
  • Providing support when you contact us
  • Maintaining App functionality, reliability, and legal compliance

Third-Party Services

The App currently uses:

  • OpenRouter: processes uploaded or captured images and prompts to generate AI-assisted reports.
  • RevenueCat: manages subscription status, purchase entitlement, and restore purchase functionality.
  • Apple services: process App Store purchases, subscriptions, camera/photo permissions, and device-level privacy controls.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Retention

Scan history and collections saved inside the App remain on your device unless you delete them, clear App data, or uninstall the App. Photos sent for AI processing are handled by the AI provider to generate the requested report. We do not operate a separate server for storing your scan photos.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through support contact or App use, please contact us so we can review the concern.

Important Disclaimer & Safety Warning

Luxury Bag Price Identifier provides AI-assisted bag valuations and details for informational and educational use only. Price estimates are for informational purposes only and are not certified appraisals, authentication results, guaranteed sale prices, or official valuations. Results are estimates and may vary depending on camera quality, bag condition, lighting, market fluctuations, and available online references. Always verify item details and values independently.
