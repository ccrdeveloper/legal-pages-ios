1. Information We Collect

GlowStyle Ideas may collect or process the following types of information depending on how you use the app:

  • Photos you choose to scan or upload for cosmetic appearance and style insights.
  • Generated scan results, such as face shape estimates, appearance notes, style ideas, and self-care suggestions.
  • App usage information, such as feature interactions, app performance, crash logs, and diagnostic data.

2. Photo Processing

GlowStyle Ideas uses selected photos only to generate cosmetic appearance and style insights requested by you. Photos may be processed by an AI service provider to create your selected scan result.

You should not upload photos of other people without their permission. You should also avoid uploading sensitive, private, or inappropriate images.

3. No Face Recognition or Identity Matching

GlowStyle Ideas does not identify people, does not perform face recognition, and does not compare faces for identity matching. The app does not predict or classify sensitive personal traits such as ethnicity, attractiveness, emotions, or identity.

We use information to:

  • Manage premium features, subscriptions, and purchase restoration.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Comply with legal, safety, and platform requirements.

5. Progress Journal

If you save scan results, the app may store journal entries so you can review previous cosmetic scans and style insights. The Progress Journal is for personal cosmetic review only. It is not medical history and should not be used for diagnosis, treatment, or health decisions.
