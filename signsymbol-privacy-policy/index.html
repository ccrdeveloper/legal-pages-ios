App Purpose

The App helps users scan signs, symbols, labels, warning marks, public icons, road signs, packaging marks, technical symbols, and similar visual markings. The App uses AI-assisted image analysis to provide educational explanations, including likely symbol name, category, meaning, reading guidance, context, confidence, readability, image quality, and useful notes.

Photos and AI Processing

When you capture or upload a photo for analysis, the photo is sent securely to our AI service provider, OpenRouter, only to generate the requested sign or symbol report.

We do not use the App to identify private people, perform face recognition, predict age, gender, ethnicity, or authenticate identity. Avoid uploading photos that contain sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for the sign or symbol explanation.

Information Stored on Your Device

The App may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Saved sign and symbol scan reports
  • Photos attached to saved history entries
  • Notes, favorites, dates, and scan history
  • App preferences and onboarding status
  • Free scan count, premium status, and related subscription state

You can clear saved symbol history from the App settings when that option is available.

Purchases and Subscriptions

The App may offer premium features such as unlimited AI scans, detailed reports, full history access, reference guides, and sharing. Purchases and subscriptions are processed through Apple and managed with RevenueCat. We do not directly collect or store your full payment card details.

How We Use Information

We use information only to provide and improve requested App features, including:

  • Generating AI-assisted sign and symbol reports
  • Saving scan history on your device
  • Managing free scan limits and premium access
  • Providing support when you contact us
  • Maintaining App functionality, reliability, and legal compliance

Third-Party Services

The App currently uses:

  • OpenRouter: processes uploaded or captured images and prompts to generate AI-assisted reports.
  • RevenueCat: manages subscription status, purchase entitlement, and restore purchase functionality.
  • Apple services: process App Store purchases, subscriptions, camera/photo permissions, and device-level privacy controls.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Retention

Scan history saved inside the App remains on your device unless you delete it, clear App data, or uninstall the App. Photos sent for AI processing are handled by the AI provider to generate the requested report. We do not operate a separate server for storing your scan photos.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through support contact or App use, please contact us so we can review the concern.

Important Disclaimer

Sign & Symbol Identifer provides AI-assisted sign and symbol explanations for educational use only. Results are estimates and may vary depending on camera quality, cropping, lighting, local rules, language, location, industry standards, and context. Always follow official posted instructions, safety labels, product documentation, professional guidance, and local regulations.
